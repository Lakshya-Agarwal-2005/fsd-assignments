Good to know

Do I need to book?

Not for the arcade floor. Console lounge stations can be booked at the counter or by phone.

Is there an age limit?

Everyone is welcome until 9 pm. After 9 pm on Friday and Saturday it is 16 and over.

Can we host a birthday?

Yes. The party room fits 15 people and comes with 200 tokens and snacks.
